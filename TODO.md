
- ROM library: persist imported ROMs and render each as its matching official-art cartridge; select a cartridge to prepare or launch that game. No cartridge before import.
- Connect progress, achievements and update notifications to real services.
- In-game save and reload test on Black 2 and White 2; gamepad test.
- Deliver emulator frames to the partner over the network session.
- Package the emulator core per platform; macOS build of the host.
- Run library: several runs and games side by side; the setup scene and play view take a run, not a single ROM.

- Main menu music: a now-playing selection from the soundtracks of the ROMs in the library (Black 2 / White 2, and other games once their ROMs are added), played from each ROM's own sound data through an NDS sequence player (SDAT: SSEQ, SBNK, SWAR); fades into the background in play.
- Friends: a friends list for finding partners, after rooms connect.
- Portraits for the Elite Four and Iris (their class graphics in a/2/9/1 have no head); battle sprites need the multi-cell layout (RECN/RNAN) assembled.
- Live encounters and areas from the game, to replace manual tracking.
